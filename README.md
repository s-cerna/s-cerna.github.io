# Sebastian Cerna — Remote CAD Drafting

Portfolio site for **Sebastian Cerna Shintani**, a remote CAD drafter producing permit-ready drawing sets for U.S. contractors, builders and homeowners — barns, decks, retaining walls, remodels and small additions, drawn to U.S. codes and delivered as PDF + DWG.

**Live site:** https://s-cerna.github.io

## About the work

Send a sketch, a marked-up PDF, or site photos, and receive a fully dimensioned, submittal-ready set. Over the past year I've drafted 50+ CAD sets for a Colorado engineering company. Flat fee, typical turnaround 3–5 business days, revisions included.

*Drafting services only — not engineering. Where a jurisdiction requires it, structural elements are stamped by a licensed Professional Engineer, arranged on the client's end.*
