How it works

Step 01

Send your sketch

A sketch, marked-up PDF, or site photos with rough dimensions — plus where it's being permitted.

Step 02

I draft the set

Flat fee or hourly, depending on scope. Typically 3–5 business days. You get a fully dimensioned, permit-oriented set.

Step 03

Delivered + revised

Delivered as PDF. DWG billed separately. 2 revisions included — additional revisions billed separately.

Scope

Drafting services only — not engineering. Where your jurisdiction requires it, structural elements are stamped by a licensed Professional Engineer, arranged on your end. I deliver a clean set ready for that review.
